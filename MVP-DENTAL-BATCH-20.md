## Current regulatory/compliance context

The MVP distinguishes two separate matters.

### HOF litigation

On 2026-08-19 the TRF1 8th Panel concluded judgment of case `1003948-83.2019.4.01.3400`, concerning CFO Resolution 198/2019 (Harmonização Orofacial). Current reporting indicates a majority decision against that 2019 resolution, while publication/procedural developments and appeal remain relevant.

### CEOF framework

The surgical-education offer modeled here is separately based on the 2026 CEOF framework:

- CFO-SEC-285/2026 amended the earlier facial-surgery prohibition framework;
- CFO-SEC-286/2026 recognizes Cirurgia Estética Orofacial (CEOF), lists CEOF procedures and defines formation requirements;
- CFO Technical Note 001/2026 reinforces that specialty competence must follow the specific rule for that specialty rather than being extended by analogy;
- PDL 177/2026 seeks to suspend CFO acts 283-286 and was still pending on 2026-08-21.

SearchLeads therefore does **not** treat the HOF judgment by itself as proof that CFO-SEC-285/286 are suspended, and it does not provide legal advice. Instead it separates lead preparation from campaign sending:


## MVP findings

1. A 20-candidate cross-region batch can be assembled from public evidence without broad crawling.
2. CRO claims and professional contact/booking channels are common in high-fit public profiles.
3. Explicit current learning intent remains sparse; it should remain a priority signal, not an outreach prerequisite.
4. Same-name matching is unnecessary for this batch; exact CRO remains the stronger dedupe key when present.
5. Some high-fit professionals are instructors/mentors. Discovery should record an educator signal for later commercial deprioritization, but this is not a launch blocker.
6. Individual CFO verification is now the binding gate for preparation readiness.
7. Campaign-level compliance remains a separate send gate because the current regulatory environment is contested and changing.
