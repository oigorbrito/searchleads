## Confirmed ICP V1

Commercial target is the professional (`Person`), with clinic/company only as context/evidence.

### Geography

- Brazil-wide by default.
- Configurable by macro-region: North, Northeast, Central-West, Southeast, South.
- Configurable by state.

### Target professional

- General dentist.
- Dentist with specialty/title.
- Oral and maxillofacial surgeon (Bucomaxilofacial).
- Professionals with evidence of HOF/facial aesthetics/facial surgery/facial-procedure activity.

### Offer fit

Training/education related to:

- Blepharoplasty.
- Lip Lift.
- Facelift.
- Frontoplasty / forehead lift.
- Related facial surgical/aesthetic procedures.

Offer format can combine in-person, immersion, mentorship, longer formation/specialization, online and hybrid formats.

### Qualification semantics

- `FIT` and `INTENT` are separate.
- Profession/profile fit alone never implies learning intent.
- Missing intent evidence remains `UNKNOWN`; it does not automatically disqualify.
- Company size is not a person-level gate for this ICP.
- Public professional contact is useful but separate from fit/intent.

### Offer tracks

- `CEOF_SPECIALIZATION`: formation/specialization prospect path.
- `COMPLEMENTARY_EXCLUSIVE_CEOF`: complementary education for CEOF-exclusive procedures; official CEOF specialty evidence is required.
