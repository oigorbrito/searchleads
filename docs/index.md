## Authority

- `CANONICAL` documents govern product and engineering decisions.
- `EXPERIMENTAL / DECISION EVIDENCE` documents record bake-off findings and may inform canonical decisions, but do not override canonical baseline.
- `REFERENCE` documents provide context only.
- `SUPERSEDED` documents are historical and should not govern new work.
